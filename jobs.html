Job Recruitment

Harvest International School seeks passionate, qualified staff with high integrity, dedication and commitment.

Teacher responsibilities

  • Prepare and deliver stimulating, clear lessons.
  • Support learners who need additional help and challenge those who are ahead.
  • Maintain a safe, healthy classroom environment.
  • Keep student development, attendance and related records.
  • Participate in meetings, training and education initiatives.
  • Maintain strong parent relations.

Qualifications & competencies

  • BSc/BA with Education and relevant school experience, or a related bachelor's degree with formal experience working with children.
  • Excellent communication and instructional skills.
  • Strong understanding of child development and educational methods.
  • A creative mind balanced with practical acumen.
